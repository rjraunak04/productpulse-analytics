# Business case

## Scenario

ProductPulse represents the analytics function of a digital commerce product. The team has event-level behavioural data but needs a consistent way to turn those events into product decisions.

## Core business problem

Growth cannot be managed from revenue alone. The team needs to understand how acquisition quality, onboarding behaviour, product engagement and retention interact before deciding where to invest product and marketing effort.

## Decisions this project must support

1. Which acquisition sources bring users who activate and retain, not merely visit?
2. Where is the largest meaningful drop in the product journey?
3. Which early behaviours are associated with stronger retention?
4. Are product experiments producing statistically and practically meaningful changes?
5. Do experiment gains come at the cost of guardrail metrics?
6. Which customer segments create sustainable value?
7. Where should product teams investigate churn risk?

## Stakeholders

**Product manager** — activation, feature adoption, retention and experiment decisions.

**Growth manager** — acquisition quality, conversion and customer economics.

**Analytics lead** — metric definitions, data quality, reproducibility and statistical validity.

**Leadership** — a compact view of sustainable growth and business impact.

## Success criteria

The finished project must let a reviewer trace a business question from raw events to a tested metric and a decision-oriented output. Results must be reproducible from documented queries/code, and simulated experimental data must never be presented as observed randomized evidence.

## Non-goals

This is not another sales dashboard, a Power BI showcase, a generic churn-model notebook, or an attempt to recreate a full commercial product-analytics platform.
