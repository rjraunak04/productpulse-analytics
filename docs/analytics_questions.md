# Analytical question backlog

## Acquisition
- Which source/medium combinations generate high-quality downstream behaviour?
- Does traffic volume translate into activation and purchase behaviour?

## Journey
- What is the observed sequence from session to product interaction to purchase?
- At which transition is the largest absolute and relative drop-off?
- How does conversion differ by device, source and cohort?

## Retention
- How quickly does repeat activity decay after first observed activity?
- Which early behaviours are associated with stronger later retention?
- Are acquisition cohorts improving or deteriorating over time?

## Experimentation
- Is assignment balanced?
- Is the observed uplift distinguishable from sampling variation?
- Is the effect large enough to matter to the business?
- Did a primary-metric gain damage a guardrail metric?

## Economics
- Which segments combine conversion, retention and value?
- Where do high acquisition volumes fail to create downstream value?

These questions are hypotheses and decision prompts, not pre-written findings. Conclusions will only be added after the data is analysed.
