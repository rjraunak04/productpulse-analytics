# Delivery roadmap

## Step 1 — Foundation
Business case, stakeholders, scope, metric framework, architecture and repository conventions.

## Step 2 — GA4 data foundation
Connect/query the public GA4 BigQuery sample, profile schema and create reproducible extraction/staging queries.

## Step 3 — Data quality
Implement event, identifier, timestamp and duplicate checks with machine-readable outputs.

## Step 4 — Behavioural layer
Create stable event, session and user analytical grains.

## Step 5 — Product metrics
Implement validated North Star and supporting product/growth KPIs.

## Step 6 — Funnels
Build configurable ordered conversion funnels and drop-off analysis.

## Step 7 — Cohorts and retention
Create cohort tables, retention curves and behavioural comparisons.

## Step 8 — Experimentation
Add reproducible treatment assignment simulation plus SRM, uplift, confidence intervals, effect size, power and guardrails.

## Step 9 — Unit economics
Connect acquisition and customer behaviour to revenue/value metrics using only defensible source fields or clearly documented assumptions.

## Step 10 — Churn and survival
Analyse time-to-inactivity/churn and segment differences; predictive modelling remains secondary.

## Step 11 — Decision application
Build a compact interactive product analytics interface around validated outputs.

## Step 12 — Release
Tests, CI, documentation, screenshots, recruiter walkthrough and v1.0.0 release.
